Telugu → English live subtitles (bookmark, version 6)

This runs inside YouTube itself. It reads auto-generated Telugu captions and shows English over the video. No extension needed.

v6 major fix:
YouTube auto-captions come out word-by-word. Previous versions translated every tiny piece → English became gibberish.

Now it accumulates the growing phrase and only translates when a reasonably complete chunk is ready. Much cleaner flow.

Step 1: copy the code

Step 2 (Android Chrome): save it as a bookmark

  1. Tap ⋮ → ☆ to bookmark this page.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Bookmarks, tap ⋮ next to the bookmark you just made, then Edit.
  3. Change the name to tren. Delete the address and paste the code in its place. Save.

Step 3: use it

  1. In Chrome (not the YouTube app) open the movie at youtube.com or m.youtube.com.
  2. Tap the address bar, type tren, and tap the suggestion with the star ☆ icon.
  3. English subtitles appear over the video. Run it again to switch off.

On a PC

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar, open the movie on YouTube, then click it: tren
